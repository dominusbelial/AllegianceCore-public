Portable AllegianceCore player bundles for Linux x64 and Windows x64.

Extract the entire archive and run `allegiance.sh` (Linux) or `play.bat`
(Windows). .NET, native FreeAllegiance artwork and the sky pack are included.
Read `README-PLAYERS.txt` for server/pilot configuration, system libraries and
troubleshooting. F1 shows help; F6 rebinds controls.

Verify the archive against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Windows builds are unsigned.
These are client bundles; obtain a server/lobby address from your operator.
