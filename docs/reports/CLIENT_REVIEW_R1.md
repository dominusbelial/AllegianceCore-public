## Summary

### Part A · five priorities

1. **Fix the missing-smoke crash first (S1).** *(Measured)* A missing Cloud01 atlas reached a modulo by zero on the first eligible puff and escaped Draw. Correcting the content root loaded 64 frames and flight continued. Select the existing fallback before constructing the enhanced renderer, and dispose frames from a partial upload. `Rendering/VfxAssets.cs:87–117`, `Rendering/EnhancedSmokeField.cs:125–133`.
2. **Reuse the particle geometry and staging buffers (P1).** *(Measured)* A Modern sector with 54 ship poses, 96 bolts and four blasts allocated 1.37 MB per rendered frame on the game thread, up from 0.49 MB in the modest sector. Vertex, puff, billboard and index arrays accounted for about 47 MB of allocation samples over 180 frames. *(Estimated)* Reusing storage inside the existing draw order is the best steady-state performance candidate. `Rendering/EnhancedSmokeField.cs:92–154`, `Rendering/ParticleBillboardBatcher.cs:95–105,218–219,293–301`.
3. **Move cold asset preparation out of the flight frame (P2).** *(Measured)* Individual planet/rock loads took 67–87 ms on software DesktopGL. *(Read)* The warm-up queue checks its time budget between items. Decode and upload need separate timings and ownership before that budget can constrain a single expensive item. `Rendering/SectorWarmup.cs:190–218`, `Rendering/PlanetPoster.cs:164–199`.
4. **Make crowded label placement cheaper without moving any labels (P3).** *(Measured)* The same 72 labels needed 0.12 ms spread out and 1.37 ms coincident. *(Estimated)* A spatial index can skip most rectangle comparisons while preserving candidate order and final placement in both HUDs. `Rendering/HudTextLayout.cs:19–51,58–91`.
5. **Separate expensive reads from the shared simulation and receive locks (P4/S3).** *(Read)* Spectator snapshots traverse all sectors under the embedded tick gate. Seated rendering resolves art under a different lock and reads some sector data outside it. Measure wait and hold times independently, then publish coherent frame inputs. Live contention and transition failure frequency remain unquantified. `SP/SkirmishObservation.cs:70–136`, `SP/SkirmishHost.cs:160–174`, `Game1.FlightInput.cs:2872–2951`, `Game1.Scene.cs:66–70`.

### Part B · three priorities

1. **Fix weapon-row and target/gauge collisions on master (B1).** *(Measured)* Flat Modern reproduces both failures. Give the status bar and mount text a common final rectangle and keep target text clear of own gauges. The two KB readings belong to different ships, and identical gun names can represent different mounts. Preserve both. `Rendering/HudRenderer.cs:2125–2147`, `Rendering/ModernHudRenderer.cs:305–326,361–395`.
2. **Give Modern separate VR instrument groups in the fork (B2).** *(Read)* The latest comparison already extracts a 10 m central Classic flight group for every skin. Modern still projects the rest of its HUD on one low panel. *(Estimated)* Separate comms, target, map and systems tiles would recover the most readable area without changing flat placement. `Z:Game1.VrHud.cs:353–360,425–429,581–598`, `Z:Vr/VrHudZones.cs:32–53`.
3. **Size actual glyphs by angle in each eye (B3), then improve contrast where needed (B4).** *(Measured)* Chat and bracket labels disappear against hulls and blasts and at oblique angles in the mirror. *(Read)* Current bracket code already requests a 0.6° floor.
