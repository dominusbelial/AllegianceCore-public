## Five strategy priorities

**1. Give an armed wing enough time to reach its target.** *(Measured)* In both multi-sector 10v10 matches, each side eventually saw a station, yet neither match produced a finale commit. In the 60-pilot game, one side had nine armed bombers against four required. All nine were still in two other sectors. *(Estimated)* Let each commander launch an ordinary strike as soon as its side has a known target, a usable route and enough purchased ordnance. This gives the wing flight time while the other side is still exploring and leaves the finale countdown available for closing the game. `small-a.log:352288,415653,445972`, `small-b.log:438650,439721,545535–545537`, `large.log:1272043–1272048`, `BotManager.Finale.cs:139–184,1024–1086`.

**2. Reserve a force that can buy, arrive and survive.** *(Measured)* At one late staging sample, nine reserved pilots per side translated into one and four armed pilots, zero assembled and zero escorts. In the actual 5v5 skirmish, siege pilots on both sides had targets and their shops repeatedly refused strike fits. *(Estimated)* Choose the wing from granted damage, a feasible dock/route and observed opposition. The marginal fourth missile and the one pilot who can screen its carrier have different value on a quiet lane and a defended one. `small-a.log:550279–550280`, `offline-err.worker-1.log:6924–6951`, `BotManager.StrikeMass.cs:169–248,400–442`, `ShowcaseFinale.cs:50,139–150`.

**3. Count bombers that can deliver the first salvo.** *(Read)* Standard and cloak holds target 3,500 and 5,000 units from the station, with a further 3,600 units allowed for assembly. A member is marked spent at release, before an accepted launch. *(Measured)* The SRM Anti-Base 2 granted in the spider match had a 1,000-unit lock range. In the winning Brawl the first effective station hit came about 33.5 seconds after go. One bomber still had full owner-reported lock on an enemy ship immediately before release. *(Estimated)* Stage by the granted weapon's firing envelope. Track each pilot's target, accepted launches and time to first hit. `catalog-run.txt:18–19`, `paced.log:274878–274920,300368`, `paced-observations.json:3–5`, `BotManager.StrikeMass.cs:400–442,752–841`, `src/Allegiance.Core/Sim/Ship.cs:4839–4875`, native `Igc/magazineigc.cpp:127–148`.

**4. Recover an ordinary raid even if the first pilot never arrives.** *(Measured)* Three simulated strike pilots continued receiving gather orders at 181 and 360 simulation seconds. Moving one pilot inside the gather radius caused an insufficient-volley abort on the next call. *(Read)* The zero-arrival return precedes the timeout. *(Estimated)* Detect lack of progress, re-scout or choose a reachable rally, and give the freed pilots another attempt while research continues. `laws-fixed.txt:10–14`, `ResearchTactics.cs:503–548`.

**5. Keep the wing fighting after its first sortie.** *(Read)* In a two-side game, the finale can retain its first destroyed target. After a failed wave, re-forming depends on a fresh pilot outside the previous member IDs, yet those IDs still qualify for release after the old go time. *(Measured)* Two podded pilots in the winning Brawl relaunched and received the old release again. *(Estimated)* Close each sortie on an observed outcome and assign rearmed pilots to the next known objective. Test an expansion and a garrison in the same sector, and two lives with the same ship ID. `paced.log:303239–303240,307715–307716`, `BotManager.Finale.cs:358–385`, `BotManager.StrikeMass.cs:279–316,444–465,752–791`, `StrikeVolley.cs:369–375`.

**1. **2. Build tactical contacts once per distinct client view.** *(Read)* The research loop visits the same shared tables through each pilot, copies contacts into a combined array and deduplicates after the copies.
