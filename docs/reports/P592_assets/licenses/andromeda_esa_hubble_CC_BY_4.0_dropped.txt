P592 GIANT SKY OBJECT — ANDROMEDA (M31) over Alderan — ATTEMPTED, DROPPED

Image obtained for evaluation: NASA, ESA, B. Williams (University of
Washington) — ESA/Hubble heic2501a (PHAT + PHAST), the full-disk Andromeda
mosaic, 42208x9870 px, CC BY 4.0 (https://esahubble.org/copyright/).
Source: https://esahubble.org/images/heic2501a/

DECISION: This image was NOT shipped as a giant object. The ultra-deep PHAST
mosaic resolves Andromeda's own disk into individual stars across the faint
halo and outer disc, indistinguishably mingled with foreground Milky Way stars.
Achieving a clean (zero-star) look requires destroying the galaxy's resolved
structure — the exact Sombrero failure the task warned about. Per the task
instruction ("if M31 can't reach zero stars without damage, report it and
leave Alderan without a giant"), Alderan has NO giant. The 330 MB source is
kept for reference; the processed sprite was deleted.
