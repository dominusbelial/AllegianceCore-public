## Native intent

**Design:** M4 permits either any miner's destruction or selected-target departure. M8 permits outpost victory, bomber loss and combined sacrifice, with independently evaluated announcements in timed goals. Those conditions, texts, weapons and encounter recipes remain intact.

**Era constraint:** the tutorial uses fixed actor identities, live combat and short narrative windows. The native shared monotonic clock assumes those systems advance together. Its 4 FPS clamp is a debug-training convenience, not shipping gameplay.

**Bug:** applying the debug clamp in the shipping port was a production timing bug. The input binding, sampled aiming and announcement repair-budget defects are corrected. M4 still has an unresolved effective-hit/retreat gap; the successful native-combat kills establish reachability, not reliability.

**Modern approach:** an explicit shared simulation clock, frame-independent input sampling and read-only emission prediction make this proof easier to reason about. They improve the harness and clock fidelity without changing native combat balance or lesson conditions.
