### F1 — Squad-game side membership and initial squad establishment are bypassed on logon/side-select and squad change

- **Severity:** High (authority bypass — unauthorized players can join enemy/rival squad teams in a squad game)
- **Confidence:** High
- **Flaw:**
  1. `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Lobby.cs:4527,4544,4639` + `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Squads.cs:91-99` (`CompleteSideSelection` calls `TryJoinSide` → `EstablishSideSquadFromLeader` before `_peerShips[from]` is registered in `CompleteJoinAndLaunch`).
  2. `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.SideIntegrity.cs:20-21,108-111` (`CheckPositionRequestCore` checks `ship is not null && ConnOf(ship) is { } memberPeer && !PeerIsMemberOfSquad(...)`, which is skipped when `CheckInitialPositionRequest` passes `ship: null`).
  3. `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Squads.cs:137-140` (`ApplySideSquad` skips booting whenever `squads is null || squads.Count == 0`, exempting verified lobby accounts with zero squad memberships).
- **Entry points:** Inbound `FM_C_LOGONREQ` + `FM_C_POSITIONREQ` (`Server.Dispatch.cs:113,968` → `CompleteSideSelection`), and inbound `FM_CS_SET_TEAM_INFO` (`Server.Dispatch.cs:1873` → `HandleSetTeamInfo` → `ApplySideSquad`).
- **Sequence & effect:**
  1. **Initial leader's squad is lost:** In a `SquadGame`, Squad Alpha's leader connects (`LogonRequest`) and sends `PositionReq` for empty Side 0. `CheckInitialPositionRequest` passes (`CanLead` is true via `peer`), then `CompleteSideSelection` (`Server.Lobby.cs:4527`) calls `TryJoinSide` → `EstablishSideSquadFromLeader` (`Server.Squads.cs:91`). Because `_peerShips[from]` is not inserted until `CompleteJoinAndLaunch` (`Server.Lobby.cs:4639`), the `_peerShips` scan finds no connection for `leaderShipId` and sets `side.SquadId = null`, leaving Side 0 open to anyone.
  2. **Rival/non-member joins occupied squad side:** Even after Side 0 has `side.SquadId = "squad-alpha"` (e.g. set via `FM_CS_SET_TEAM_INFO` by the leader), a rival player from Squad Bravo (or a squadless player) connects (`LogonRequest`) and sends `PositionReq(sideId: 0)` (or `PositionReq(-1)` auto-pick). `CheckInitialPositionRequest` passes `ship: null` into `CheckPositionRequestCore`; at `Server.SideIntegrity.cs:110`, `Count(0) > 0` enters the occupied-side branch, where `ship is not null` is `false`, completely skipping `PeerIsMemberOfSquad` and launching the rival directly onto Squad Alpha's team (with full team chat, visibility, and ship access).
  3. **Squadless verified accounts survive squad boots:** If a verified lobby account with `Squads = []` (`Count == 0`) is seated on Side 0 and the leader sends `FM_CS_SET_TEAM_INFO` to assign a squad, `ApplySideSquad` (`Server.Squads.cs:138`) hits `if (squads is null || squads.Count == 0) continue;` and leaves the non-member seated.
- **Native behaviour:** Native registers the `CFSPlayer` before `RequestPosition` → `CheckPositionRequest` / `AddPlayerToSide` (`FedSrv/fsmission.cpp:4708-4721, 661-690`), so the first leader's `GetPreferredSquadToLead()` immediately sets the side's squad, every joiner onto an occupied squad side is checked with `pfsPlayer->GetIsMemberOfSquad(pside->GetSquadID())` (`DPR_WrongSquad`), and `SetSideSquad` (`fsmission.cpp:1623-1641`) boots every player on the side for whom `!pfsPlayer->GetIsMemberOfSquad(squadID)` regardless of whether their membership list is empty.
- **Suggested fix:** Resolve the joining peer in `CheckPositionRequestCore`'s occupied-side check via `peer ?? (ship is not null ? ConnOf(ship) : null)`; reconcile `EstablishSideSquadFromLeader` once `_peerShips[from]` is registered in `CompleteJoinAndLaunch` (just like `StartCommand` at `Server.Lobby.cs:4652-4658`, or pass `ConnectionId?` into `TryJoinSide`); and in `ApplySideSquad`, only exempt genuinely unverified/bot peers (`!_peerIdentities.ContainsKey(kv.Key)`), booting verified accounts whose `Squads` list is empty or lacks `squadId`.

### F2 — Non-artifact treasure spawns ignore `SeenBySide`, leaking unseen enemy drops and ambient spawns across the sector

- **Severity:** Medium (information / fog-of-war authority leak)
- **Confidence:** High
- **Flaw:** `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Treasure.cs:11-31` (`BroadcastGeneratedTreasure`, `ArtifactKnownToPeer`), `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Treasure.cs:45-48` (`SendTreasureSnapshot`), and `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Docking.cs:497-506` (`SendTreasureSpawnSectorWide`)
- **Entry points:** Inbound `FM_CS_DROP_PART` (`Server.ShipCommands.cs:718`), `FM_CS_RELOAD` cargo overflow (`Server.ShipCommands.cs:2615`), ship death / CTF flag drops (`Server.Combat.cs:2259,2282,2308,2327,2431`), and periodic ambient treasure generation (`Server.Lifecycle.cs:921` → `BroadcastGeneratedTreasure`).
- **Sequence & effect:**
  1. Player A (Side 0) sits docked at a station or flies near one edge of Sector X, with no Side 0 eyes anywhere near the opposite side of Sector X.
  2. Player B (Side 1, e.g. a stealth bomber, carrier, or miner 15 km away in Sector X, unseen by Side 0) sends `DropPart` (`FM_CS_DROP_PART`), overflows cargo on `Reload`, dies and drops parts/cash/a CTF side flag, or an ambient powerup/cash/development treasure spawns in unseen space in Sector X.
  3. Both `SendTreasureSpawnSectorWide` and `BroadcastGeneratedTreasure` gate recipients with `ArtifactKnownToPeer(treasure, ship)`, which evaluates `treasure.Code != TreasureCode.Flag || treasure.Data.TreasureId != Side.ArtifactSentinelFlag || ...`. Because that expression is `true` for every non-artifact treasure, the server immediately sends `FM_S_TREASURE` (`TreasureSpawn`) to Player A containing the exact `P0` position, `V0` velocity, `TreasureCode`, `TreasureId`, and `Amount` — revealing both the unseen treasure and the exact 3D coordinates/velocity of the unseen enemy ship that dropped it, and latching `_crossSideDiscoverySent`. (Separately, `SendTreasureSnapshot` at `Server.Treasure.cs:48` uses `side is not { } s || t.SeenBySide(s)`, exporting all treasures including unseen artifacts if a peer's `Ship.SideId` is `null`.)
- **Native behaviour:** Every `CtreasureIGC` — part, development, cash, powerup, CTF side flag, or artifact — is created with `c_mtSeenBySide | c_mtPredictable` (`Igc/treasureIGC.cpp:167-177`). Native has no sector-wide creation broadcast; `ShowObject` (`FedSrv/FedSrv.CPP:10750-10778`) exports `OT_treasure` to `CFSSide::FromIGC(pside)->GetGroup()` only when `SeenBySide(pside)` becomes true, and `fsmission.cpp:4224-4231` exports only `ptlink->data()->SeenBySide(pside)` to a joining player.
- **Suggested fix:** Gate all treasure exports on `peer.SideId is >= 0 && treasure.SeenBySide(side)` (refreshing the new treasure's side visibility against its sector on creation, or letting `Server.Broadcast.cs:2281-2302`'s per-tick discovery sweep deliver it to all peers on seeing sides), and change `SendTreasureSnapshot` to require `side is >= 0 && t.SeenBySide(s)`.

### F3 — Cross-sector boarding + unconditional observer boarding enable instant remote capital-ship/bomber teleportation and strand boarded gunners' sector state

- **Confidence:** High
- **Flaw:**
  1. `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Docking.cs:71-74,124-126` (`PerformBoard` collapses native's `if ((turret != NA) || pfsShip->GetPlayer()->CanCheat())` observer gate to unconditional `true`).
  2. `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Docking.cs:194-198` (`PerformBoard` sets `boarder.StationId = cs` without moving `boarder` to `carrier.Sector` or updating `_peerShips[boarderConn].Sector`), combined with `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.ShipCommands.cs:2657-2664` (`ApplyLoadout` searches all `mission.Sectors` for `ship.StationId`) and `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Docking.cs:2288-2326,2362` (`UndockShipState` searches only `live.Sector.Stations` and launches in `live.Sector`).
  3. `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Docking.cs:1592-1650,2530-2541` and `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Combat.cs:2930-2955` (`MoveShipToSector`, `UpdatePeerSectorSnapshot`, `PerformTeleport`, and `OnShipWarped` never propagate sector changes or `SetCluster` bursts to `ship.ChildShips`).
- **Entry points:** Inbound `FM_C_BOARD_SHIP` (`Server.Dispatch.cs` → `PerformBoard` / `PerformLeave`), `FM_C_BUY_LOADOUT` (`ApplyLoadout`), `FM_C_DOCKED` (`PerformUndock` / `PerformTeleport`), `FM_C_RIPCORD_REQUEST` (`PerformRipcordTeleport`), and aleph transit (`OnShipWarped`).
- **Sequence & effect:**
  1. **Remote capital-ship / bomber teleportation & station-tech bypass:** Player A flies a scout to any friendly station in forward `SectorAlpha` (e.g. a Teleport Receiver or captured Outpost with no Starbase/Garrison tech) and docks (`boarder.Sector = SectorAlpha`, `liveA.Sector = SectorAlpha`, `boarder.Position` at `Station1`).   3. Player A sends `BuyLoadoutRequest(HullId = Battleship/Bomber/HTT, Parts = ..., FLaunch = true)`. `ApplyLoadout` (`Server.ShipCommands.cs:2657-2664`) looks up `Station2` across `mission.Sectors`, passes `StationSellsHull` against home Starbase, mounts the capital/bomber loadout, and calls `PerformUndock`. `UndockShipState` (`Server.Docking.cs:2293-2321`) looks up `Station2` only in `liveA.Sector.Stations` (`SectorAlpha`), finds `stationModel = null`, skips `ChangeStation` and `RepositionForLaunch`, clears `StationId`, and sends `SetCluster(SectorId = SectorAlpha.Id)` at Player A's existing coordinates in `SectorAlpha` — instantly spawning a `HabmNoRipcord` Starbase hull in `SectorAlpha`.
  4. **Stranded turret gunners on carrier sector transitions:** Whenever a carrier with boarded children warps (`OnShipWarped`), ripcords (`PerformRipcordTeleport`), teleports between stations (`PerformTeleport` with `!bNewHull`), or is boarded cross-sector and undocks, `ship.ChildShips` keep their old `Ship.Sector` and `_peerShips[childConn].Sector` (and receive no `SetCluster` on warp/ripcord), so boarded gunners continue receiving broadcasts for the old sector and cannot see ships in the carrier's actual sector. - **Suggested fix:** Gate `turret is null` observer boarding in `PerformBoard` on `CanUseAdminCheats(boarderFrom, boarder)`; in `PerformBoard` (when `carrier.Sector` is non-null) and `PerformTeleport`, move `boarder` / `ChildShips` to the target station's sector and update `_peerShips[conn].Sector`; resolve `UndockShipState`'s station from `ship.Mission.Sectors` (moving `ship` + `ChildShips` + `_peerShips` to that station's sector if ever mismatched); and make `MoveShipToSector` / `OnShipWarped` / `PerformRipcordTeleport` propagate sector membership, `_peerShips[childConn].Sector`, and `SetCluster` to `ship.ChildShips` (and filter `PerformLeave` by mission).

### F4 — Server-authorized repositions (`PerformRelaunch`, `PerformRipcordTeleport`, `RelocateShipToSector`) omit `NextCookie` and/or `SetCluster`, freezing relaunched players and stranding ripcorded clients

- **Severity:** High (client/server authority desync — carrier landing permanently locks out player flight updates; cross-sector ripcord leaves client in old sector)
- **Confidence:** High
- **Flaw:**
  1. `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Docking.cs:1468-1479` (`PerformRelaunch` hardcodes `RelaunchShip.Cookie = 0` instead of rotating `NextCookie(EnsurePeerUpdate(landerConn))`).
  2. `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.Docking.cs:553-676` (`PerformRipcordTeleport` moves `rip` to `targetSector` and calls `UpdatePeerSectorSnapshot`, without sending `SetCluster` + `SendTreasureSnapshot` or calling `NextCookie(EnsurePeerUpdate(from))`).
  3. `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.AdminCheats.cs:322-326,357-380` (`HandleRelocateCheat` / `RelocateShipToSector` checks only `target.Sector is null` — missing docked/boarded/`SideTeamLobby` ships whose `Sector` stays non-null — and moves `target` across sectors without sending `SetCluster` or calling `NextCookie`).
- **Entry points:** Carrier bay landing collision (`ServerHost` → `PerformRelaunch`), inbound `FM_C_RIPCORD_REQUEST` countdown completion (`PerformRipcordTeleport`), and inbound admin `relocate` chat cheat (`HandleRelocateCheat`).
- **Sequence & effect:**
  1. **Carrier landing freezes player flight:** A human player with a non-zero `PeerShipUpdate.Cookie` (e.g. `1` at join, `2` after undock) lands on a friendly carrier. `PerformRelaunch` repositions the ship at `+50 m/s` and broadcasts `RelaunchShip` with `Cookie = 0`. In `ClientWorld.Apply(RelaunchShip)` (`src/Allegiance.ClientCore/State/ClientWorld.FlightInterface.cs:85`), `message.ShipId == MyShipId` overwrites the client's own ship `Cookie` with `0`, while `EnsurePeerUpdate(landerConn).Cookie` on the server stays `2` (and `HasMovementAnchor` is not rebased). Every subsequent `FM_C_SHIP_UPDATE` from that player hits `EnsurePeerUpdate(from).Cookie != u.Cookie` (`Server.Dispatch.cs:465-478`) and is dropped whole (controls included) until the player dies/ejects or hits an aleph.
  2. **Ripcord omits `SetCluster` + `NextCookie`:** A human player sends `FM_C_RIPCORD_REQUEST` for another sector (or a distant anchor in the same sector) and completes the countdown. `PerformRipcordTeleport` moves `rip` to `targetSector` on the server, but sends no `SetCluster` (nor `SendTreasureSnapshot`) and does not rebase `HasMovementAnchor` via `NextCookie`. - **Native behaviour:** Native `LandOnCarrierEvent` (`FedSrv/FedSrv.CPP:13015-13016`) stamps `if (pfsShip->IsPlayer()) pfmLaunch->cookie = pfsShip->GetPlayer()->NewCookie();` on `FM_S_RELAUNCH_SHIP`, and native `UseRipcord` (`FedSrv/FedSrv.CPP:13442,13482` → `FedSrv.CPP:13527` → `FedSrv/fsship.cpp:747-930`) routes through `pship->SetCluster(pcluster)` → `CFSPlayer::SetCluster`, rotating `NewCookie()` and sending `FM_S_SET_CLUSTER` + cluster state to the ripper and its children.
- **Suggested fix:** In `PerformRelaunch`, resolve the owning player connection (`ConnOf(lander)`) and stamp `Cookie = NextCookie(EnsurePeerUpdate(landerConn))`; in `PerformRipcordTeleport` (and `RelocateShipToSector`), emit `SetCluster` with `NextCookie(EnsurePeerUpdate(conn))` + `SendTreasureSnapshot` to the ship (and `ChildShips`), and reject `HandleRelocateCheat` when `target.StationId is not null || target.ParentShip is not null || target.SideId is not >= 0 || target.Dead`.

### F5 — `FM_C_FIRE_EXPENDABLE`, `FM_CS_FIRE_MISSILE`, and `FM_C_TREASURE_ACK` bypass mount-readiness, dispenser cooldowns, ripcord restrictions, and server missile-lock geometry

- **Severity:** High (combat authority bypass — 0-tick multi-pack mine/probe/chaff dump, instant 0s in-space part mounting, unmounted missile firing, and instant 360° full-sector missile lock)
- **Confidence:** High
- **Flaw:**
  1. `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.ShipCommands.cs:766-812` + `src/Allegiance.Core/Sim/Ship.Equipment.cs:111-174` (`HandleFireExpendable` → `FireDispenser` omits `UpdateDispenser`'s `MountedFraction >= 1`, `now > part.TimeLoaded`, `!chaff && RipcordModel is not null`, `!Dead`, and `SideId >= 0` checks, while synchronously calling `TryAutoReloadLauncher` when `remaining == 0`).
  2. `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.ShipCommands.cs:872-907` + `src/Allegiance.Core/Sim/Ship.cs:4922-4943` (`TryFireMissile` skips `launcher.MountedFraction < 1f` when `clientRequest == true`, unconditionally overwrites `launcher.TimeLoaded = now + missileType.LoadTime` even on a dud shot during mounting, accepts client `req.Lock` instead of server `MissileLock`, and omits `!Dead && SideId >= 0`).
  3. `src/Allegiance.Server/Server.ShipCommands.cs:2565-2588` (`HandleTreasureAck` mounts freshly picked-up parts at `ExpandedPartData.FullyMounted` instead of `0`, and searches only `ship.Parts` — which excludes `_cargo` — before calling `MountPart` on cargo mounts).
- **Entry points:** Inbound `FM_C_FIRE_EXPENDABLE` (`HandleFireExpendable`), `FM_CS_FIRE_MISSILE` (`HandleFireMissile`), and `FM_C_TREASURE_ACK` (`HandleTreasureAck`).
- **Sequence & effect:**
  1. **Single-packet full-cargo mine / probe / chaff dump:** Whereas the held-key `StateM` path (`UpdateDispenser`, `Ship.Equipment.cs:96-101`) enforces `part.MountedFraction >= 1`, `now > part.TimeLoaded`, and `(!chaff && RipcordModel is null)`, explicit `FM_C_FIRE_EXPENDABLE` calls `FireDispenser` directly without any of those checks. Worse, when `remaining == 0`, `FireDispenser` (`Ship.Equipment.cs:172`) synchronously calls `TryAutoReloadLauncher(now, part.EquipmentType)` on the server, swapping the next cargo pack into mount 0 (`MountedFraction = 0`, `TimeLoaded = now + 1/MountRate + loadTime`). A single UDP packet bundling e.g. 30 `FireExpendableRequest` frames dumps the ship's **entire equipped + 5-slot cargo load** of mines, probes/EWS/gun-towers, or chaff in 0 ms on a single tick — even immediately after undocking or while mid-ripcord.
  2. **Missile mount-delay shortcut via sacrificial dud shot:** After undocking, swapping a rack from cargo (`SwapPart`), or auto-reloading (`TryAutoReloadLauncher`), `launcher.MountedFraction` is `0` and `launcher.TimeLoaded` is `now + 1/MountRate + LoadTime` (typically ~9–12s). Sending one `FireMissile` immediately fires a single dud (`now + 0.5 < TimeLoaded`) and **overwrites `launcher.TimeLoaded = now + missileType.LoadTime`** (`Ship.cs:4940`) while `MountedFraction` is still `0`. Waiting `LoadTime - 0.5s` (often ~0.5–1.5s) and sending `FireMissile` again yields `dud == false`, firing live armed missiles ~7–8s before the rack finishes mounting (and firing every subsequent missile `0.5s` faster than `LoadTime` because `TimeLoaded` is re-anchored to `now`).
  3. **Instant 360° unlimited-range 100% missile lock:** In `TryFireMissile` (`Ship.cs:4941-4942`), `clientRequest: true` uses `Math.Clamp(req.Lock, 0f, 1f) * missileType.MaxLock` instead of the server's per-tick cone/range-computed `MissileLock` (`UpdateMountedMissileLock`, `Ship.cs:4892`). A modified client can select any visible enemy anywhere in the sector (100 km away or directly behind the ship) and immediately send `FireMissile { Lock = 1.0f }` for full-lock tracking without ever pointing at or closing range on the target.
  4. **Instant 0s combat part mounting via `TREASURE_ACK` (and lost cargo-stack merges):** Accepting a floating part into an active slot (`mount >= 0`) via `FM_C_TREASURE_ACK` calls `ship.MountPart(..., ExpandedPartData.FullyMounted)` (`Server.ShipCommands.cs:2583`), instantly arming shields, weapons, cloaks, boosters, or launchers in space without the ~8–10s `1 / MountRate` delay (e.g. drop + re-pickup or teammate drop). Conversely, accepting a stackable pickup into a partially-filled same-type cargo slot (`sbyte` `-1..-5`) searches `ship.Parts` (`_parts` only, `Ship.cs:989`), misses `_cargo`, hits `MountPart`'s occupied-cargo refusal (`Ship.cs:3423`), and permanently destroys the already-cleared `PendingTreasureOffer`.
- **Native behaviour:** Native's `CdispenserIGC::Update` (`Igc/dispenserigc.cpp:85-119`) and `CmagazineIGC::Update` (`Igc/magazineigc.cpp:85-185`) enforce `m_mountedFraction == 1.0f`, `m_timeLoaded < now`, non-chaff `GetRipcordModel() == NULL`, and cone/range lock accumulation on the client (though native `FedSrv.CPP:5433-5508,5634-5780` lacked server-side dispenser cooldown/mount checks and trusted client `pfmFireMissile->lock`, without server-side `TryAutoReloadLauncher`). On `FM_C_TREASURE_ACK` (`FedSrv/FedSrv.CPP:6117-6124`; `Igc/shipIGC.h:866,1490-1513`; `Igc/shieldIGC.cpp:70-84`), `CreateAndAddPart` leaves `m_mountedFraction = 0.0f` (only station `BuyLoadout` calls `ppart->Arm()`), and `GetMountedPart` checks `m_mountedCargos` when `mount < 0`.
- **Suggested fix:** Gate `HandleFireExpendable` / `FireDispenser` on `!Dead && SideId >= 0 && part.MountedFraction >= 1f && now + 0.5 >= part.TimeLoaded && (part.EquipmentType == EquipmentType.ChaffLauncher || !IsRipcordActive)` (advancing `TimeLoaded` from `Math.Max(now, part.TimeLoaded) + loadTime`); in `TryFireMissile`, require `!Dead && SideId >= 0`, treat `launcher.MountedFraction < 1f` as `dud = true` without shortening an existing future `launcher.TimeLoaded`, and bound `requestedLock` against server-tracked `MissileLock`; and in `HandleTreasureAck`, pass `fractionMounted: 0` for `signedMount >= 0` and look up `_cargo` when `signedMount < 0`.
